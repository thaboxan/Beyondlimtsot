About Beyond Limits OT

Meet Onwabile Stemela

Beyond Limits Occupational Therapy is a private practice in East London and Mthatha that renders a multitude of services both in Medico-Legal field and Clinical settings.

The Founder, Onwabile Stemela, has vast experience in government and private sector. She has been practicing as an Occupational Therapist for more than 10 years. She holds a BSc degree in Occupational Therapy and several Certifications including being certified as a Workwell Practitioner.

We are committed to delivering therapy that is both effective and meaningful to each client's personal journey.

About Beyond Limits OT

Beyond Limits Occupational Therapy is a private practice in East London that renders a multitude of services both in Medico-Legal field and Clinical settings.

Founded by Onwabile Stemela, a qualified Occupational Therapist with over 10 years of experience in both government and private sectors. She holds a BSc degree in Occupational Therapy and certified as a Workwell Practitioner.

Our Services

⚖️

Medico-Legal Services

RAF, Disability evaluations, functional capacity evaluations, 3rd party claims, personal injury claims, and insurance claims.

🏥

Acute Therapy

In-hospital therapy for CVA, spinal cord injuries, hand injuries, burns, amputations, and orthopedic injuries.

🏠

Clinical Services

Home safety modifications, assistive devices, functional mobility improvement, and independence in daily tasks.

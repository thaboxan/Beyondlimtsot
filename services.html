Our Services

Medico-Legal and Clinical Occupational Therapy Services

⚖️ Medico-Legal Services

Our medico-legal services provide comprehensive evaluations and expert reports for legal proceedings. We work with attorneys, insurers, and employers to assess the impact of injuries and illnesses on function and daily living.

As a certified Workwell Practitioner with over 10 years of experience, we deliver thorough, evidence-based assessments that stand up to legal scrutiny.

  • RAF Assessments
  • Disability Evaluations
  • Functional Capacity Evaluations
  • 3rd Party Claims Assessment
  • Personal Injury Claims
  • Medical Negligence Reports
  • Insurance Claims Assessment
  • Assessment of injury/illness on function

🏥 Acute Therapy

We provide acute occupational therapy services in private hospitals for patients with various conditions. Our in-hospital therapy focuses on early intervention and rehabilitation to help patients regain function and independence.

Working closely with the medical team, we ensure comprehensive care that addresses all aspects of the patient's recovery journey.

We treat conditions as listed below but our intervention is not limited to the conditions below:

  • CVA (Stroke) Rehabilitation
  • Spinal Cord Injuries
  • Hand Injuries
  • Burns Treatment
  • Trauma Related Injuries
  • Amputations
  • Orthopedic Injuries
  • Rheumatoid Arthritis
  • Conditions affecting daily living, work, and leisure

🏠 Clinical Services

Our clinical services focus on improving patients' independence in daily activities and overall quality of life. We assess, treat, and provide recommendations to help patients function at their best in their home and community environments.

We work with patients to identify barriers to independence and develop practical solutions that make a real difference in their daily lives.

  • Treatment of loss of function
  • Improve independence in performing tasks
  • Ordering assistive devices (wheelchairs, commodes, etc.)
  • Home Safety Modifications advice
  • Improve Functional Mobility
  • Activities of daily living assessment

Who Can Refer?

Doctors, Allied Professions (Physiotherapists, Speech Therapists, OT's), Lawyers/Attorneys, Employers, and Insurers can all refer patients to our practice.

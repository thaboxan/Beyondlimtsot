Our Services

Comprehensive occupational therapy for every stage of life

👶 Pediatric Therapy

Our pediatric occupational therapy services are designed to help children reach their full potential. We work with children of all ages who may be experiencing developmental delays, sensory processing challenges, or learning difficulties.

Through play-based therapy and family-centered care, we help children develop the skills they need to succeed at home, school, and in their communities.

  • Developmental delay assessment and intervention
  • Sensory processing therapy
  • Fine and gross motor skill development
  • Handwriting and school readiness programs
  • Social skills training
  • Parent education and support

🏃 Adult Rehabilitation

Our adult rehabilitation services focus on helping individuals recover function and independence following illness, injury, or surgery. We work closely with clients to develop personalized treatment plans that address their unique goals and challenges.

Whether you're recovering from a stroke, managing a chronic condition, or adapting to life changes, we're here to support your journey to independence.

  • Stroke rehabilitation
  • Post-surgical recovery
  • Chronic pain management
  • Neurological condition support
  • Activities of daily living training
  • Adaptive equipment recommendations

💼 Workplace Wellness

Our workplace wellness services help organizations create healthier, more productive work environments. We provide comprehensive assessments and interventions designed to prevent injury, reduce stress, and support employees returning to work after illness or injury.

Investing in workplace wellness leads to happier, healthier employees and a more successful business.

  • Ergonomic workstation assessments
  • Return-to-work programs
  • Stress management workshops
  • Injury prevention training
  • Disability management consulting
  • Corporate wellness programs
